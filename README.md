Empirical study on whether M&A activity affects corporate non-financial disclosures (NFDs), using a 2010–2024 panel of 20,935 M&A events matched with Refinitiv Eikon ESG data. OLS with firm and year fixed effects tests M&A's impact on ESG Combined Score, Environmental Score, and CO2 emissions, controlling for board diversity, board independence, profitability, and capital expenditure. A robustness check interacting M&A with the EU's Non-Financial Reporting Directive (NFRD) shows the positive M&A–ESG relationship is largely driven by post-NFRD regulatory pressure rather than M&A activity alone.
